🔍 Official Government Document & Credential Verification Console

Authenticate any issued Indian digital credential against the National Blockchain Registry. Government authorities can audit using either the applicant's Public SHA-256 Hash or by uploading the Official Signed PDF.

🔑 Enter Citizen's Public SHA-256 Hash

Query the immutable blockchain ledger using the 64-character hexadecimal SHA-256 cryptographic digest.

📄 Full Document Cryptographic Verification

Upload the citizen's PDF document to recalculate binary SHA-256 hash and cryptographically verify the government RSA-PSS (2048-bit) signature.

📜 Direct Government Issuance

Issue an official card directly with or without an existing PDF file

Optional: Upload a pre-existing department PDF. If omitted, the system generates the official national card PDF automatically!

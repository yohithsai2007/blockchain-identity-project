💡 How to share your credentials for verification:

Option 1: 1-Click Public Hash Sharing (Fastest)

Click the 📋 Copy button next to your document's SHA-256 hash above. Send this 64-character hash to your employer or agency. They can verify your official record directly without needing your physical documents.

Option 2: Download Official Signed PDF

Download the official PDF document and provide it to the verifying authority. The document is digitally signed by the government and contains cryptographic hashes.
